What makes a strong password?

A strong password is generally long, difficult to predict, and not based on information that someone could easily guess about you. Longer passwords and passphrases can provide a larger search space.

  • Use a unique password for each important account.
  • Prefer longer passwords rather than relying only on complicated symbols.
  • Avoid names, birthdays, common words, and predictable patterns.
  • Consider using a reputable password manager to store unique passwords.
