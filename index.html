/ BUILT WITH INDIA IN MIND

Cybersecurity for a billion+ digital journeys.

India is experiencing one of the world's fastest-growing digital transformations. From digital payments and online education to businesses, creators, cloud platforms, and connected devices, more of everyday life is moving online. Kynex is built with this environment in mind — an India-focused cybersecurity initiative that aims to encourage stronger cyber awareness, responsible technology use, and practical digital security habits. Our goal is simple: help build a generation that doesn't just use technology, but understands how to use it safely.
